# Outro

The signature ending of every reel. Keep the sign-off wording identical every time.

## Words

> Comment **KEYWORD** and I'll send you the links. This is your AI guy.

- `KEYWORD` changes per reel (REPO, PHONE, VOICE ...). Everything else stays.
- The ask comes first, the sign-off last. Never put a second ask after it.

## Timing

- The whole outro sits in the last 3 to 5 seconds.
- "This is your AI guy." is the very last line, about 1.2 seconds.
- The last frame flows back into the reel's first frame so a replay feels seamless.

## On screen

- "Comment" above a huge keyword tile in the reel's accent colour.
- Under it: *This is your* **AI guy.** — italic serif, with "AI guy." bold in the accent colour.
- The avatar centre-bottom at about 60% of the frame width, looking at the viewer.
- All text above the bottom 420 px of a 1080 x 1920 frame, clear of Instagram's buttons.
- See `../backgrounds/*-outro.jpg` for the layout in each background.

## Behind it

The keyword triggers Meta Business Suite's free comment-to-message automation,
which DMs the links. Add the keyword in its common spellings (REPO, repo, Repo,
repos, REPOS). Pin a comment with the links as a backup.
