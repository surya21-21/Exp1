# Pack: character (R01-R15)

The avatar drives the story. A paper puppet with a jointed body walks, points, reacts, sits on the cards, uses props and rides the transitions, and every finding is something he does. Code: `studio/packs/character.js` and `studio/packs/character/` (`rig.js`, `act.js`, `props.js`, `scenes.js`, `transitions.js`). Props and beats: `studio/packs/character.md`. Demo pictures: `collection/demo/character/` (rebuilt with `python3 studio/packs/character/make_demo.py build/reel.timed.json`).

All numbers are at 30 frames a second. S is the head image's box in px. Every value is a function of time; frames drawn in any order are identical (checked byte for byte).

**Owner to approve:** the body is a new part of the character (the heads are the approved brand images and are never redrawn, recoloured, stretched or mirrored: uniform scale and rotation only). See `demo/character/r01-puppet.jpg`.

## Limits, stated plainly

- He always faces the camera (the heads are front views and are never mirrored); walking sideways is shown by the legs, arms, lean and head tilt.
- Short arms on a big head: anything raised above the shoulder passes in front of the face, so points are capped at 35° above level and "One" is a finger beside the cheek.
- The `curious` face exists only small and `interested` and `thinking` only large; a forced face is mapped to the nearest one in the set in use.
- No 3D: he is drawn in 2D and must touch things precisely, so this pack uses no three.js scene.
