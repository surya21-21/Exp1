# Motion pack (G01-G15)

Kinetic type, operated interfaces and carries: what the motion-graphics pack (`studio/packs/motion.js`, `studio/packs/motion/`) adds to the engine. Every entry is our own code, drawn by the engine in Paper Studio materials, and each one starts from a measured recipe or a named principle. Frame counts are at 30 frames a second. Demo strips (rendered by our engine from the reel `2026-11-15-12-reels-b`) are in `../demo/motion/`.

The one idea under all of it: **carry, not replace.** At every scene change something of the old scene becomes something of the new one (T01-T24 "patterns": carry; skill rule "Carry, do not replace").

| G01 | `morphCarry`, shared-element morph | Elements with the same `data-morph` name in the old and the new scene are measured every frame; a flyer takes over from the old one and travels on a shallow arc to the new one's box, changing width, height, corner radius and paint while its contents cross over. Pairs leave 2 frames apart (the stagger shrinks so each flight lasts at least 8 frames). Travel: 2.5 frames of pull-back by 5% of the distance, accelerating to the half-way point (inCubic), then outBack(1.6): overshoot about 5%, settle. Arc bend 16% of the distance, upward. New paint fades in over 30-75% of the flight, the old paint is removed only once covered; new content in over 25-55%, old out over 42-67%. Lift: drop shadow 4/7/6 px growing to 14/27/22 px at mid-flight. Blur 0.07 x pixels a frame along the travel. | G03 | `blindFlip`, blind flip | The frame breaks into vertical strips of paper that turn over one after another, left to right; each carries a slice of the old scene on its front and of the new one on its back. | 6 strips of 180 px, window 6 + 10 frames; each turns 180 degrees in 9 frames (inOutCubic), starting 1.4 frames after its left neighbour. | G04 | `dotZoom`, dot zoom | The camera dives into the full stop marked `data-zoom` in the old scene; the dot swells until the frame is inside it, then shrinks onto the element marked `data-zoom` in the new scene (a badge), undershooting and settling, while the new scene pulls back out around it. | Lead 8 frames: push-in to 2.8x (inCubic) about the dot, the dot drifting 55% of the way to the centre, blur up to 9 px; the dot swells to cover the frame over the last 70% of the lead.
