Three pacing families:
- **Wall of words** (r04-r06): 4.25 to 4.33 words a second, never more than 0.36 s of silence. The pictures do the breathing.
- **Tutorial** (r08-r12): 3.1 to 3.7 words a second, a 0.3 to 0.5 s breath between steps.
- **Mood** (r01-r03): 1.6 to 2.9 words a second, a half-second pause after nearly every sentence, filled by sound design.

Sentences are short everywhere: the middle sentence is 6 to 9 words; the longest in any reel is 16.

## Structures (P01-P06), with where each part falls

**P01 Product pitch** (r04, r05, r06)
claim with a number (0-10%) > "and this matters because" or "but that's not even the best part" (12-20%) > reframe: "the hard part was never X" (22-32%) > the tool by name (a quarter to a third in for r04 and r06; in the first sentence for r05) > features, each one a state change on screen (35-60%) > objection removed (about 55%) > proof number or a specific tip (60-80%) > "free to try" (85-90%) > comment keyword (starts 94-95% in, lasts 1.4 to 2.2 s).

**P02 Objection ladder** (r06, also r01)
Five short questions the viewer would ask ("Don't know what to post?"), each answered in one or two sentences, 4 to 7 s apiece. The answer always starts with "It" plus a verb.

**P03 Numbered walk-through** (r09, r10, r12)
hook > "here's what you're missing" or a time promise > item: number and name (1 s), what it does, why it helps (3 to 5 s) > a re-hook about 40% in ("these next four are the ones most people miss") > "save this" > comment keyword. In settings tutorials each step is: where to go, what to switch, what you get, in that order.

**P04 Rule and example** (r02)
"a rule every [audience] needs to know" > the rule in one sentence > two short parallel lines > one named example > the number at the midpoint > why it works in three very short sentences > a question to the viewer > a two-word command > a last line that ends on the title phrase.

**P05 Community invite** (r01)
the feeling > "so we built a place" > the name > who it is for > two question-and-answer pairs > what is coming > an honest line (it is new, the first members are still to come) > invitation > comment keyword.

**P06 Show, don't tell** (r03, r11)
problem > "watch this" > three or four labelled demos with little or no speech > one line of proof > a closing saying.

## Our script rules, taken from this

1. First word on frame 0; 8 to 12 words in the first 3 seconds; the tool and the payoff in sentence one.
2. Aim for 3.2 to 3.7 words a second for tutorials, 4.0 to 4.3 for pitches. No silence over 0.5 s unless a sound demo fills it.
3. Sentences of 6 to 9 words; one two-word sentence per reel.
4. A re-hook near 40% in any reel over 45 s.
5. "Save this" at 50 to 65% (r10 places it right before the ask; ours stays mid-reel so it does not compete with the keyword).
6. Outro: "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the links. This is your AI guy." About 3.5 s.
